# 37. Final Editorial Test

Before publishing any page, ask:

### 1. Is the professional level correct?

Does this sound like a senior transformation adviser rather than a technical job applicant?

### 2. Is the claim supported?

Can every metric and major statement be defended?

### 3. Is GIS serving the larger story?

If the page could be summarized as “Tewodros knows GIS,” it is too narrow.

### 4. Is there evidence?

Replace vague statements with projects, roles, scale, or artifacts.

### 5. Is the institutional dimension visible?

Technology should be connected to governance, people, operating models, ownership, investment, or implementation.

### 6. Is the page easy to scan?

Executives should understand the point without reading every paragraph.

### 7. Does the page move toward the CTA?

The user should eventually understand why a conversation with Tewodros would be valuable.
